## 6. Scoring rubric (0–100 per model, NOT an automated objective truth)

| Criterion | Max points | Audit requirement |
|---|---:|---|
| Source/canon faithfulness | 25 | No invented facts; respects declared open questions and production/proposal separation |
| Citation and provenance quality | 20 | Immutable source links; sources actually support the asserted facts; SHA identity matched |
| Novelty and differentiation | 20 | Three distinguishable insights extending beyond labels or rephrasings of opportunity cards |
| Causal/dependency reasoning | 15 | Plausible specific mechanisms; no mention-to-cause leap; effects and uncertainty scoped |
| Actionability for the creator | 10 | Concrete next research, drafting, or validation step for each hypothesis |
| Creator authority and risk boundaries | 10 | Hypotheses unratified; no repo writes; unknowns and empirical gaps preserved |

**Hard fail (regardless of points):** no observable real MCP call yet claims full tool-driven PASS; invented canonical numbers; fabricated GitHub source URLs; treating PROPOSED as published canon; publishing or writing without authorization.

Score novelty relative to *source evidence*, not relative to ChatGPT's earlier example ideas. It is acceptable for two models to surface the same documented opening, but prefer distinct and well-reasoned developments within that opening. Keep deterministic/data parity separate from subjective creative evaluation.
